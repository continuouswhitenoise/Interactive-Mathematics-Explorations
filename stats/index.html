What to notice

As n increases, the theoretical sampling distribution becomes narrower and taller around the population mean. The standard error decreases according to

σ / √n

The magenta curve updates continuously during animation. To keep the animation responsive, the random simulation is recalculated when the animation ends, or when you manually select a sample size. The population itself does not change unless you generate a new sample.
