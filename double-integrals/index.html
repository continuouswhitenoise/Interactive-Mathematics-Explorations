Double integrals begin with Riemann sums

We approximate a double integral by adding contributions from many small pieces of a region.

\[ \sum_{i=1}^{m}\sum_{j=1}^{n} f(x_{ij}^{*},y_{ij}^{*})\,\Delta A_{ij} \]

Each sample value is multiplied by the area of its small piece. The double integral is the limit of these sums as the pieces become arbitrarily fine.
