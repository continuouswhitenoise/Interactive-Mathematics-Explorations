The Law of Large Numbers

Because a fair coin has two sides, every single toss carries a 50% probability. While a few quick flips rarely split the proportion perfectly down the middle, expanding the experiment changes everything. Flip the coin a large number of times, and the resulting proportion mathematically approaches 50%.
