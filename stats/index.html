What to notice

As n increases, the theoretical sampling distribution becomes narrower and taller around the population mean. Its standard error decreases according to

σ / √n

The histogram is a genuine simulation: each sample mean is calculated from its own sequence of random population observations. During animation, those sequences grow as n increases, allowing the mint bars to evolve without restarting the entire simulation every frame.
