# Double Integral Visualizer: Iterated Integrals

An interactive educational tool for exploring how to construct iterated integrals over special regions to evaluate double integrals.

## Overview

This simulation helps students visualize the relationship between a region in the plane and the corresponding iterated integral. By exploring different orders of integration, students can develop a geometric understanding of how the bounds of integration describe a region.

The goal is to make setting up double integrals more intuitive by connecting the geometry of a region with its mathematical representation.

## Learning Goals

- Visualize regions in the plane.
- Understand how bounds of integration describe a region.
- Explore the construction of iterated integrals.
- Compare different orders of integration.
- Develop geometric intuition for evaluating double integrals.
