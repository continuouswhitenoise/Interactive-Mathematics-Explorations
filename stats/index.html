What should you observe?

As n increases, the sampling distribution becomes narrower and taller. The sample mean has expected value μ and standard error σ/√n. Its values therefore concentrate around the population mean as n grows.

The magenta curve is the normal density with mean μ and standard deviation σ/√n. The Central Limit Theorem explains why the distribution of standardized sample means approaches a standard normal distribution under its usual assumptions.

In the limit, the unstandardized sample mean converges in probability to μ. Its distribution concentrates at a single point. The standard normal limit, by contrast, describes the sample mean after subtracting μ and dividing by its standard error.
