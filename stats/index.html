What should you notice?

1. The population stays non-normal. Increasing the sample size does not change the original population distribution.
2. The sample means become more concentrated. The standard error is σ/√n, so larger samples produce means that tend to lie closer to the population mean.
3. The shape approaches a normal distribution. Under the usual conditions of the Central Limit Theorem, the sampling distribution of the mean approaches normality as n increases. It is not generally perfectly normal for finite n.
