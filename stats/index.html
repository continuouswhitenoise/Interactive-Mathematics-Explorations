What to notice

As n increases, the theoretical sampling distribution becomes narrower and taller around the population mean. Its standard error decreases according to

σ / √n

Compare a reflected exponential population (left-skewed), a normal population (symmetric), the original exponential population (right-skewed), and a randomly distorted population built from a randomly weighted mixture of different distribution shapes. Each population has its own mean and standard deviation, which determine the theoretical curve. Changing the population shape generates a new population and fresh sample means. During animation, each simulated sample grows as n increases.
