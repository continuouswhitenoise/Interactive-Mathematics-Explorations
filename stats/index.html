What should you notice?

1. The population does not become normal. The original distribution remains exponential or uniform.
2. The sample means concentrate. Their standard deviation is σ/√n, so larger samples produce means that tend to lie closer to the population mean.
3. The shape approaches normality. Under the usual conditions of the Central Limit Theorem, the sampling distribution approaches a normal distribution as n increases. It need not be exactly normal for finite n.
