What should you notice?

1. The population does not become normal. The original distribution remains exponential or uniform.
2. The sample means concentrate. Their standard deviation is σ/√n. Increasing n narrows the sampling distribution around the population mean.
3. The shape approaches normality. Under the usual conditions of the Central Limit Theorem, the sampling distribution of the sample mean approaches a normal distribution as n increases. It need not be exactly normal for finite n.
4. The pink curve is theoretical. It is the normal approximation, not a curve fitted to the simulated histogram.
